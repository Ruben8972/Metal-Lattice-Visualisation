Defect Types in Crystal Structures (FCC, HCP, BCC)

1. FCC (face-centered cubic)
----------------------------
- Stacking fault + Shockley partials on {111} (must-have):
  * Stacking fault: Local disruption of the layer stacking order (for example, ABCABC -> ABCA BC).
  * Shockley partials: Partial dislocations with Burgers vector b = a/6<112>, moving on {111} planes.
- Twin on {111}:
  * Twin: Mirroring of a crystal region across the {111} plane.
- Edge/screw dislocations (b = a/2<110>) as a reference:
  * Edge and screw dislocations with the full Burgers vector for comparison with partial dislocations.

2. HCP (hexagonal close-packed)
-------------------------------
- Basal stacking faults (I1/I2/E):
  * Stacking defects in the basal plane (0001).
  * I1, I2, and E represent different stacking sequences.
- Twinning on {10-12} (tension):
  * Tensile twin on the {10-12} plane, typical under tension along the c-axis.
- a-type dislocation on the basal plane:
  * Burgers vector b = a/3<11-20>, lying in the basal plane.

3. BCC (body-centered cubic)
----------------------------
- Screw dislocation b = a/2<111> (qualitative display of its non-planar character):
  * Screw dislocation with a core distributed across multiple slip planes.
- Edge dislocation for comparison:
  * Edge dislocation as a reference case.
- Optional: twinning band {112}<111>:
  * Twinning bands on {112} planes with direction <111>.
